# Retina specialty workflow review

The Retina clinic now receives the published `Retina assessment` examination template automatically for new, follow-up, and general visits. Dr. Hamza Ali can receive Retina appointments through the normal intake and workup flow. Existing signed records remain linked to their original template version.

## Implemented workflow

- Bilateral retinal diagnosis context, systemic risk, previous treatment, vitreous, macula, vessels, peripheral retina, and optic-disc findings
- OCT macula status, central subfield thickness, retinal fluid, fundus imaging, angiography, and per-eye imaging summaries
- Disease severity, activity, progression, and urgent complication assessment
- Observation, intravitreal injection, laser, surgery, and urgent-referral planning with explicit laterality
- Follow-up interval, next investigation, safety-netting, and role-specific team handoff
- Expanded fundus markers for microaneurysms, cotton-wool spots, neovascularisation, and retinal detachment
- Longitudinal Retina monitoring in Patient 360 alongside visual acuity, IOP, refraction, findings, diagnoses, and drawings

## Hospital clinical review checkpoint

Before production use, the hospital's retina lead should review:

- Diagnosis, severity, activity, progression, OCT, fluid, and complication option lists
- Required fields and which disciplines may edit each field
- OCT thickness limits, terminology, and imaging workflow
- Injection, laser, surgery, and urgent-referral documentation requirements
- Recall intervals, safety-netting wording, and required investigations
- The retinal marker catalogue, drawing labels, and anatomical conventions

Approved changes should be delivered as a new published template version. Published version 1 is immutable so signed encounters retain the exact structure used at the time of care.
